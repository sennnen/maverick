# ADR-015 — Stream kinds for the WHOOP gen5 deep buffers (v20 optical, v21 IMU)

## Context

The WHOOP 5.0/MG R22 deep buffers carry two raw high-rate offloads the frozen `StreamKind`
vocabulary could not name. The offsets are corpus-pinned from `tanarchytan/whoop-rs` `[WRS]`, but no
real fill has been captured on our own hardware — a full deep buffer needs the R22 stream running on
a worn, subscribed band — so both decoders are `[PROV]`/UNVERIFIED and are pinned by synthetic
invariant tests (ADR-009's second bar: property tests that can genuinely fail), not golden fixtures:

- **v21 IMU** — 100 samples per strap-second of 6-axis motion, columnar `i16` LE. The accelerometer
  maps onto the existing `StreamKind::Imu`, but the gyroscope has no kind; folding it into `Imu`
  would erase the accel/gyro distinction (different physical units, g versus deg/s).
- **v20 optical** — ~25 samples per second across six photodiode channels, each a 20-bit signed ADC
  count in a 4-byte LE word. `StreamKind::Ppg` is explicitly single-wavelength; a six-channel raw
  optical buffer is a different signal and must not be conflated with the cardiac PPG waveform.

Both records are gated on structure, not the version byte: the IMU on its two in-packet sample
counts (both 100) and the optical on the green-LED echo anchor (`inner[23] == 2 × inner[20]`), so a
mis-routed or short frame decodes to nothing rather than to plausible garbage.

## Decision

Add two additive `StreamKind` variants: `Gyro` (raw 3-axis gyroscope, `seq = sample * 3 + axis`) and
`OpticalRaw` (raw multi-channel optical, `seq = channel * samples_per_channel + sample`). The v21
decoder emits accelerometer samples as `Imu` and gyroscope samples as `Gyro`; the v20 decoder emits
every channel's 20-bit-sign-extended counts as `OpticalRaw`. Values stay raw LSB/counts with no
scale applied — the physical scale (g, deg/s) is the consumer's, exactly as for the other raw
streams. No existing variant is renamed or removed, so nothing serialized changes.

Routing stays version-keyed by the manifest, unlike the upstream RE, which tries the IMU layout first
because a real deep buffer's version byte was seen to collide with v18. Maverick has no real deep
capture to confirm which version byte these carry, so it routes `20 → gen5_v20` and `21 → gen5_v21`
and relies on the internal count/anchor gates to reject a mis-route. The collision risk is recorded
in docs/protocol/whoop.md and revisited when a real fill is captured.

## Consequences

Connectors may advertise `imu`, `gyro`, and `optical_raw`, and analytics that need raw motion or
multi-channel optical have a typed home for it. The deep decoders are `[PROV]`: they reproduce a
constructed buffer exactly and reject a broken one, but no number here is hardware-verified, and the
ledger and manifests say so. The mav-sqi scorer's `StreamKind` match carries a wildcard, so both new
kinds score `unassessed` until a rule exists. The cost is two more frozen-interface members admitted
ahead of a real capture — justified only because the gates make a wrong decode fail loudly rather
than store garbage, and because the alternative (flattening gyro into accel, or optical into PPG)
would bake in a lie that a later real capture could not cleanly undo.
