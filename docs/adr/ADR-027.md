# ADR-027 — Two interval streams, and the raw channels behind them

Status: accepted.

## Context

`StreamKind::RrInterval` was the only beat-to-beat kind, and every connector emitted onto it — from
an optical pulse. "R-R" names the interval between two R waves, an electrical event, and the whole
of `docs/analytics.md` turns on the distinction between that and the pressure pulse a photodiode
sees some milliseconds later. The distinction was carried instead by an `IntervalSource` enum the
*caller* passed in, so the spine asserted `IntervalSource::Ppg` in a comment-documented convention
and nothing in the type system stopped a caller asserting otherwise.

`IntervalSource::Ecg` had no producer at all. `whoop-protocol::realtime_raw` decodes a single-lead
ECG at 100 Hz — verified on live hardware, `docs/protocol/whoop-raw-afe.md` — but `StreamKind` had
no ECG variant, the connector wire contract had no arm for one, and no connector declared it. The
one route to genuine heart-rate variability was unreachable by construction.

The same gap closed off the v0x0b pulse-ox triad: red, infrared and ambient at 25 Hz, decoded and
documented, with nowhere to go. `Spo2Raw` is one channel and oximetry needs the pair plus its
reference.

## Decision

Split the interval kinds by the physiological event that timed them, and admit the raw channels:

- `StreamKind::RrInterval` — intervals from an electrical R peak. **Only these may be labelled
  `heart_rate_variability`.**
- `StreamKind::PulseInterval` — intervals from an optical pulse; labelled `pulse_rate_variability`.
- `StreamKind::Ecg` — the single-lead electrical waveform, raw counts.
- `StreamKind::RedPpg`, `InfraredPpg`, `AmbientLight` — the oximetry triad, raw counts.

`IntervalSource` is deleted. The label is derived from the kind, so a caller cannot assert a claim
the stream does not support. `mav-analytic::ecg` detects R peaks from the waveform (Pan–Tompkins)
and the spine prefers electrical beats when a day holds both.

A connector declares which one it produces, and where it can, it reads that from the device: the
Generic HR Monitor publishes onto `rr-interval` only when the Bluetooth SIG Body Sensor Location
characteristic says the sensor is on the chest, and onto `pulse-interval` otherwise — including
when the device declines to say.

## Consequences

Additive on the wire and in storage: existing kinds keep their names and their codes, and five new
ones append. The WHOOP connectors move from `rr-interval` to `pulse-interval`, which is a behaviour
change and the point of the ADR — their fixtures regenerate and their optical beats stop being
labelled as electrocardiography.

The capability graph gains an "any of" requirement so an analytic that only needs the timing of
beats is served by either stream, while one that needs them electrical still asks for `RrInterval`
alone.
